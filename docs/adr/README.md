# Architecture decision records

An ADR in this repo records a decision that is true now — the architecture's current
contract and its stated boundaries — not a proposal, and its own text owns its status.

| ADR | Title | Status | Decision in one line |

Owner-approved extensions add the synthetic form/duplicate projection, the consent-holder and life-stage vocabulary, the source heading and entry provenance, and the display-only `Picture.intended_use`. |

## Reading order

Start with **0011**: it states the three layers, the deterministic core's authority, and the
boundary rule that every AI path reads only `Scoped`. Read **0004** next — silent absence is the
disclosure discipline everything else respects. Then 0011's neighbours: **0001**, the `Picture`
contract at the seam, and **0003**, the honest empty-evidence state (`arvioimaton`) that 0004
tightens. Read **0018** for the classifier substitution and the accepted verifier risk it records,
**0021** for why the decision layer stays a typed call, and **0027** for the tool-using assistant
that is now the conversational surface. **0016** and **0026** decide the UI's design authority and
presentation layer, and change no wire field, policy input or selection behaviour; **0029** moves
0026's pin and adopts the record-display patterns in Finnish. **0017** is
orthogonal in the same way — it adds a response marking and changes no `Picture` field, policy input
or selection behaviour. **0020** records the data-mapping direction and its refusals, **0023** and
**0024** record how proposals and urgency are kept honest, and **0025** records the authorization and
worklist direction.

**Before repeating any date, article, licence or vendor claim from an ADR, read
[`../references.md`](../references.md).** It is the primary-source citation layer for this decision log: one
table per question area, quoting what each source actually says, with a marked **Unverified** section and a
list of repository claims the sources do **not** support. Three claims depended on sources that turned out
not to support them (the 200-token short-text threshold in 0017, the cited-only grounding restriction
attributed to the platform in 0018, and "openEHR alignment" as partial conformance) and are corrected there
and in the records themselves.
